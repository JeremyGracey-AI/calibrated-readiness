# Enterprise Cloud Architect Certification (ECA-200) - Exam Blueprint

*Synthetic knowledge document for the Calibrated Readiness Foundry IQ knowledge base.*

The ECA-200 exam has 60 scored questions, 120 minutes, scaled passing score 700/1000.
Questions are distributed across five domains. The blueprint weighting below is the
authoritative source agents use to size study plans and practice sets.

| Domain | Weight | Approx. questions | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1. Identity & Access | 20% | 12 | Entra ID, managed identities, RBAC, conditional access, least privilege |
| D2. Networking | 22% | 13 | VNets, peering, private endpoints, hybrid DNS, load balancing |
| D3. Data & Storage | 18% | 11 | Storage tiers, replication, Cosmos/SQL choice, encryption at rest |
| D4. Reliability & Ops | 25% | 15 | SLOs, autoscaling, backup/restore, observability, incident response |
| D5. Security & Governance | 15% | 9 | Policy as code, key management, threat detection, compliance baselines |

## Difficulty mix
- 35% recall, 45% application (scenario), 20% analysis (multi-service trade-off).
- Domain D4 (Reliability & Ops) carries the most weight and the most analysis items;
  it is the most common reason candidates fall short.

## Cut-score notes
- The scaled cut score (700) corresponds to roughly 70% of weighted items correct,
  but exact raw-to-scaled mapping varies by form. Treat "70% weighted" as a planning
  proxy, not a guarantee.
